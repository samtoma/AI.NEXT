Grade 10 Maths book: ingestion in numbers

How the whole of Siyavula Everything Maths Grade 10 became a curriculum graph and a question bank for the American-curriculum Grade 10 course (course:us-g10-math-en): the time, the AI agents, the cost, what came out, how it was checked, and what is still to do.

Dated 2 October 2026 App version v0.11.0 (latest release) Report rev 1 Not live yet: local pilot database only

The short answer

  • Yes, the whole book is through. All 14 chapters went through the pipeline and passed the final gate (G5): 13 chapters with written holds, Chapter 11 clean. The drift guard (parity) is green.
  • What came out: 211 learning objectives across 65 lessons, joined by 294 prerequisite links, and 5,326 live questions (2,090 from the book, 3,137 generated variants, 99 interactive widget questions), plus 300 misconception explanations.
  • What it took: about $943 of AI compute (API list price), 4,394 AI agents in 280 pipeline runs, about 1.2 billion tokens. Inside the ≈ $0.85–1.1k approved for the book.
  • How long: an 18½-hour pilot on Chapter 8 (26 Sep), then the other 13 chapters in 24 hours of wall-clock time (1–2 Oct).
Read before sharing further. This content is not in front of students. It sits in a local pilot database; it is on a feature branch, not on main, and nothing is deployed. Apart from Chapter 8, every quality gate was passed by the AI line, with each decision recorded. No person has yet read the live questions: human review in the admin console is the next job.

Coverage: safe everywhere, not yet complete everywhere

Every safety check passes in every chapter (among them: the maths renders, notation is consistent, every question has a sourced solution, teacher-only text stays hidden). The completeness checks are what keep 13 chapters on "pass with holds":

  • 178 of 211 objectives (84%) have full evidence; 33 lack a book question or a cited claim.
  • 554 of 633 objective-difficulty slots (88%) have a live question; 79 are empty, mostly in Chapters 5, 6 and 10.

Each gap is either signed off by a person as an exception or filled with new generated questions or widgets: an open decision for Samuel.

Defects the line caught in itself, and fixed

  • Step titles lost their maths (138 of 571 worked-example titles): fixed at the source; runs, bundles and the database repaired with no new model calls.
  • Coordinate notation: the book writes (x; y); students now see (x, y) everywhere they read text.
  • A wrong sample space ({(1,1);(1,2)…} read as decimals) in one probability question, invisible to the notation check: found and fixed.
  • Maths that would not render in Chapter 13 (47 strings): repaired at assembly.
  • Drift guard now counts only servable questions (Samuel's answer 45); a one-objective lesson is allowed once with a "check later" note (answer 44).

Accountability

Who decided what

Samuel (a person)

  • Chapter 8 pilot: the objective gate and the question gate, 26 Sep.
  • 45 written answers that set the rules: go for the full book with auto-passing gates, book pictures as stand-in figures, quality over cost, which model, how multi-part questions read.
  • Two rule changes on 2 Oct: the drift guard counts servable questions only; one lesson may keep a single objective, marked "check later".

The AI line (agents)

  • Every gate on chapters 1–7 and 9–14, under Samuel's auto-pass rule, each decision recorded with its reason.
  • Six objectives dropped and their 78 questions moved to a neighbour, by an AI ruling that needs a standing policy.
  • Fixes to its own defects, each tested and marked in the files; none yet reviewed by a person.
Open

What is not done yet

  1. Human review in the console. The live questions, generated variants, widgets, misconceptions, the 102 errata and the 300 working-check flags have not been read by a person. This is the next job.
  2. Book-level close-out. Chapter 8's assembled content is not yet on the branch; the whole-book export, the book's status and the book-wide coverage report still have to run before the course can be loaded anywhere else.
  3. Merge, release, deploy. The work sits on a branch forked at v0.9.3; main has since released v0.10.0 and v0.11.0. A trial merge shows 15 conflicts to resolve, then a pull request, green CI, a release and a deploy on Samuel's go.
  4. Refresh stale records. The G5 records of chapters 1–4, 8 and 14 predate later fixes; the misconception drafts of chapters 1–5 were built before the question-gate recommendations. Re-running them is cheap (≈ $1.3 an objective for misconceptions) and is Samuel's call.
  5. One objective with nothing live (Chapter 8.1, "drawing figures from coordinates"): three question families are written and graded but not loaded.
  6. Paperwork. Requirement and traceability entries are owed for several rules the run introduced; three decisions (62–64) are missing from the table.

Design authority

How the pipeline was built: the Data Spine thesis

The ingestion line is not ad hoc. ADR-0001 makes The Agent-Native Data Spine — A Thesis (founder's thesis v1, June 2026; 20 chapters, about 75,000 words, 28 sources) the design authority for the solution architecture, and ADR-0005 builds the extraction pipeline on it. How its ideas show up in this run:

Pillar I: schema first, not text first
Every stage writes typed records (objectives, claims, questions with an answer spec) that are validated before they load, rather than free text.
Ch. 2–3: document understanding and deterministic extraction
The e-book is read by structure (4,599 blocks), maths images become typeset maths with an acceptance check, and assembly is plain code with no model calls; this is why the step-title defect could be repaired by a script rather than by re-running the models.
Ch. 15: curriculum graphs and educational agents
Objectives, lessons and modules with a prerequisite graph (15.1 schema); every question and claim cites the objective and the book passage it came from (15.5).
Ch. 15.7: build a spine, sell verticals
A second curriculum's book arrives as a new course beside Prep-3 on the same graph and the same gates, switched on per curriculum and grade.
Ch. 19.6: MVP-cut discipline
One chapter piloted end to end, measured, then the rest fanned out; nothing is built that the pilot did not need.
Ch. 16: bitemporal knowledge
Syllabus versioning on graph edges is the design (ADR-0001); this run loads one version and does not yet exercise it.
