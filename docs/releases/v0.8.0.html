The console knows you from Cloudflare

You already prove who you are to Cloudflare with your email and a PIN. The console now uses that proof, so there is no second password. A security review read the change before release.

Signing in to the console

New No password on admin-noor.reletix.com

What happens
You open the console, and Cloudflare asks for your email and a PIN, as it does today. The console then signs you in as the operator with that email. There is no second form.
Who gets in
Active operators whose console email is the same address you give Cloudflare. Capital letters don't matter.
What the console checks
Cloudflare's signed token: that Cloudflare issued it, for this console, and that it hasn't expired. The console never trusts a plain email header. If anything fails, nobody is signed in and the password form appears as a fallback.
What is recorded
Operator signed in, with the reason cloudflare-access and your roles, on the Security page.

New Someone Cloudflare knows but the console doesn't

What they see
A page that says this email has no console account. The console records the refusal with the address.
A shared browser
If the browser was signed in as another operator, the console ends that session. The console always follows the person Cloudflare just proved.

New Sign out means sign out

What it does
Signing out of the console also ends your Cloudflare session for the console. Your next visit asks for a new PIN, so it can't quietly sign you back in.

Also in this release

WhatWhy it matters
Hardening from the security reviewA sign-in can't be started by an image or frame on another website. Only plain-ASCII addresses are accepted. The identity check never waits more than a second. A flood of fake tokens can't fill the security log.
A local "Sign in as…" for engineersThis exists only on a developer's own machine and is locked three ways. It isn't in the live console at all, and CI fails if it ever appears there.
CI checks the Cloudflare settingsA mistyped setting stops the deploy. Before, it would have switched the feature off without saying so.
