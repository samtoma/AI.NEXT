Noor · release v0.9.0 · 2026-09-24

No more walls: the limits are watched, not enforced

A tester hit the lesson limit after ten minutes, and a third of those replies were taps on "Continue". Noor no longer stops a student for talking too much or uploading too much. The console now shows how often that happens and what it costs.

What changes for a student

Changed No limit on Noor's replies

Before
A lesson stopped after 18 replies, a review after 5, and asking Noor about a question after 2. Each time the student saw a "that's my limit" message and could not type any more.
Now
There is no limit anywhere. After 5 replies in a review, the Finish button pulses to suggest finishing, but nothing stops the student.

Changed No daily limit on photo uploads

Before
The 11th upload in a day was refused.
Now
A student can upload as much as they need. Files must still be 10 MB or smaller, and a photo (JPEG/PNG) or a PDF. Those checks keep out broken files; they do not ration use.

Changed With Socratic probing on, the answer waits for the second attempt

Before
The answer box waited for the student's second wrong attempt, but a student who asked "just tell me" could get the answer after only one.
Now
Both wait for the second attempt. Noor asks for a try first, even a guess. The AI can no longer open the box early.
Who sees it
Only students on a lesson where probing is on. Today that means test accounts only.

What the console shows

New Cost → Turn limits

What it shows
For each old limit, in the period you choose: how many conversations there were, how many reached the old number, how many went past it, and the longest conversation. The panel turns amber when any conversation reached a limit. A list links to each of those conversations.

New Cost → Photo uploads

What it shows
Uploads and how many students sent them, whether each was read successfully, and the photo-reading cost with its average per upload, kept separate from tutoring cost. It also shows the days a student sent more than 10, highlighted and listed. Nobody has uploaded yet, so the panel starts empty.

New An amber tag on sessions

Where
The student's session list, the session page and the replay, for example "Past 18 replies · 23".
Who sees names
Which conversations and which students is shown only to operators who also hold the student-data role. Billing-only operators see the totals.
