## 2. Content anatomy — what a lesson is made of

Sampled deeply: **T1 U1 L1–L3** (geography), **T2 U1 L1** (economic geography), **T1 U3 L2** الحملة الفرنسية (history), **T1 U4 L4** 1914–1952 (history), **T2 U3 L3** October war (history). Structure is strongly templated and consistent across all samples:

1. **Unit opener** (full page): «أهداف الوحدة» — 6–10 behavioral objectives («يحدد… يوزع… يقارن… يفسر…») + per-lesson thumbnails.
2. **Lesson opener** (full page) — *the extraction gift of this book*. Every lesson opens with a structured metadata panel:
   - **أهداف الدرس**: 3–11 behavioral objectives per lesson — the ministry's own LO decomposition, in assessment verbs (يحدد/يصف/يستنتج/يقارن/يذكر بنود/يقيم/يعلل). Example, T1 U4 L4 has 12 including «يذكر بنود تصريح ٢٨ فبراير ١٩٢٢م», «يقارن بين دستور ١٩٢٣ ودستور ١٩٣٠».
   - **مفاهيم أتعلمها**: named concepts introduced (e.g. الدول الجزرية، حوض النهر، مقاومة، جلاء، الحماية البريطانية، الزراعة الكثيفة).
   - **معلومات إثرائية**: enrichment topics (explicitly supplementary).
   - **خرائط / صور / رسوم بيانية**: an inventory of the lesson's figures.
   - **قضايا متضمنة / القيم المتضمنة**: embedded issues & values (affective; not assessable).
3. **التمهيد** — short narrative intro, often bridging from the previous lesson («عرفت في الدرس السابق أن…» — the book states its own dependencies).
4. **Body sections** under colored banner headings, two distinct templates:
   - **Geography template** (highly regular): per-continent or per-region blocks repeating **الموقع → الخصائص المناخية → الخصائص النباتية** (or الجبال/الهضاب/السهول for relief; أماكن انتشارها/خصائصها/أهم المحاصيل for crops). Facts are frequently *map-reading claims*: «نلاحظ من الخريطة (٤) أن قارة إفريقيا تمتد بين دائرتي عرض…». Classification trees appear as printed diagrams (e.g. أهم الموارد الزراعية → غذائية/ألياف/طبيعة خاصة).
   - **History template**: event blocks structured as **الأسباب → الأحداث → النتائج** (sometimes الشروط for treaties, الإعداد, خط السير for campaigns). Occasionally rendered as a literal 3-column cause/event/result diagram (ثورة القاهرة الأولى, T1 printed 49). Dense, dated fact sequences (T1 U4 L4 walks 1918 → 1919 → لجنة ملنر → 1921 → تصريح ٢٨ فبراير ١٩٢٢ with its 4 reservations → دستور ١٩٢٣ → 1924 → دستور ١٩٣٠ → معاهدة ١٩٣٦ terms + results → ٤ فبراير ١٩٤٢ → 1947 → 1951 → حريق القاهرة → يوليو ١٩٥٢). Enumerated treaty terms and numbered result lists are everywhere — natural question fodder. Late lessons add **شخصيات مضيئة** (notable-figure mini-bios with portraits).
5. **Inline boxes**: «مفاهيم أتعلمها» definition boxes (bordered, term + definition — clean mcq/short source); «معلومة إثرائية» sidebars (enrichment — should be tagged and excluded from core assessment or tiered `advanced`).
6. **Inline activities** (formative, unanswered): «ناقش معلمك/زميلك…», «حدد على خريطة صماء…», «لاحظ الخريطة (١٩) وحدد…». Not usable as bank questions as-is (no canonical answers), but they signal what the ministry considers practice-worthy.
7. **Lesson close**: a QR code — «توجه إلى الموقع الإلكتروني للوزارة لحل الأنشطة والتدريبات على الدرس» (elearning.moe.gov.eg).

**Two critical findings:**

- **The book contains NO printed question sets.** No lesson-end exercises, no unit reviews, no term exam models (Term 1 ends at printed 86 → straight into the Term 2 divider; Term 2 ends at printed 88 → spec page → back cover). All exercises live on the ministry e-learning site behind QR codes. Consequence for the pipeline in §4: unlike math (where 450 questions were *curated from the book*), the social-studies bank must be **authored from content + lesson objectives**, with external exam models as the calibration source. This raises the human-review burden and makes acquiring past papers/exam models a near-requirement.
- **The introduction sets an explicit examination policy** (المقدمة, PDF 4): «ملحوظة مهمة: الأرقام والنسب والسنوات بالكتاب ليست هدفًا للورقة الامتحانية وإنما للاستدلال والاستنتاج» — *figures, percentages and years are not exam targets; they are for inference and deduction.* This is an authoring-policy directive: don't drill raw statistics or date recall; questions should target explanation (بم تفسر), consequences (ما النتائج المترتبة على), comparison, and map/chart reading. How strictly this applies to landmark history dates (1798, 1919, 1952…) needs a call from Samuel/the content reviewer — real exams do still anchor on era ordering.

### 3.2 (a) LO decomposition for narrative subjects

**Use the ministry's own أهداف الدرس as the primary LO source** — they are behavioral, per-lesson, and syllabus-official (better provenance than anything we'd invent). Filtering rule:

- **Keep** cognitive objectives (يحدد، يصف، يفسر، يقارن، يستنتج، يذكر بنود، يقيم) → 1 LO each, merging trivially-overlapping ones.
- **Drop or demote** affective/values objectives (يقدر عظمة الله، يحرص على التعايش، يقدر دور…) — not machine-assessable; at most fold into the LO `description`.
- **Extract shared skill LOs** for capabilities that recur across lessons rather than duplicating them: قراءة الخريطة الصماء (blank-map reading), قراءة الرسم البياني (chart reading), قراءة/رسم الخريطة الزمنية (timeline reasoning). These become 3–5 LOs under a small "skills" module and are prerequisite sources (see 3.5).

Granularity outcome: lessons carry 5–11 printed objectives; after filtering/merging expect **3–5 assessable LOs per lesson → ~100–130 LOs** for the book (math: 90 LOs / 178 pp — same order of magnitude; narrative LOs are a bit denser per page but shallower each).

### 3.3 (b) Question types & what `canonical_solution` means here

- **`mcq` and `short` cover the subject fully.** Egyptian social-studies exam forms — اختر، أكمل، ما المقصود بـ، بم تفسر، ما النتائج المترتبة على، من صاحب هذا الدور، صواب/خطأ مع التصويب، matching — all reduce to mcq (incl. 2-choice true/false; matching decomposed into single mcqs) or short (تفسير/تعريف/استنتاج with a model answer). **`numeric` will be ~unused** (legitimately: a handful of map-coordinate or area questions could be numeric, but the intro's exam-policy note argues against drilling figures — recommend simply not producing numeric items).
- **`canonical_solution` = evidence chain, not derivation.** Proposed convention (fits the existing `solution: list[str]` with no schema change):
  - Each step is **one atomic claim, in Arabic, traceable to the cited page** — e.g. for «بم تفسر فشل الحملة الفرنسية على الشام؟»: step 1 = the Ottoman-British-Russian alliance claim, step 2 = the resistance of عكا under أحمد باشا الجزار with British naval supply, step 3 = the conclusion sentence that answers the stem.
  - The final step restates the complete model answer; `answer` holds the concise form.
  - `source_note` names the section header the claims come from (e.g. «الحملة الفرنسية على الشام — النتائج»), so the review UI and the tutor's grounding slices can point at the exact block, not just the page.
  - For mcq, at least one step must state *why the correct choice is correct*; distractor plausibility is a review-gate concern (see 3.4).
- **Grading implication (flag for backend/ai-engineer):** `short` answers in Arabic are judged by the existing LLM comprehension-grading path, but the rubric must be "matches the model answer's claims", never "is factually true in general" — grounding discipline is the product's trust story, and doubly so on politically sensitive lessons (T2 U3: Arab–Israeli conflict, 2011/2013; T1 U2 L1: السلالات البشرية). An off-book "correct" fact here is a parent-trust incident even if true.

### 3.4 (c) Verification without arithmetic — proposed protocol

Math's `verified=true` meant *an independent agent re-solved from the stem and matched*. There is nothing to re-solve here, so redefine verification for narrative subjects as **independent grounded cross-check**:

1. A second agent (separate context, no access to the author's bundle) receives **only the stem + the cited source page image(s)** and answers independently.
2. It then checks the canonical answer claim-by-claim: **every claim in every solution step must be visible on the cited page** (or explicitly within the lesson's page range for synthesis questions, with each claim's page listed). The verifier must quote the supporting book phrase per claim — span-grounding, exactly the thesis's citation discipline.
3. For mcq additionally: confirm **no distractor is also true per the book** for the asked stem (the classic authored-mcq failure mode).
4. `verified=true` only if the independent answer matches AND all claims ground. Mismatches follow the math pipeline's discrepancy path (fix or drop; 0-unresolved rule stands).

**Honest limitation:** this is *agreement + grounding*, weaker than arithmetic re-solving as proof — two vision models can misread the same scanned line. Mitigations: mandatory quoted spans (a fabricated quote is checkable by a human in seconds), and the human review gate remains the real trust boundary (per CLAUDE.md rule 3 it was never optional anyway). Worth recording as the subject-specific verification standard in the ADR that approves this extraction (proposal: fold into an "ADR-000X: second subject onboarding" decision).

**New pipeline validation checks** (coordinate with qa-engineer, extend the §4 deep-dive checks): claim-grounding required for `verified`; *figure-dependent stems must link a `visual`* (a question that says «من الخريطة المقابلة…» with no attached visual is invalid — new referential check in `schemas.py` is trivial: if stem matches a figure-reference pattern, `visuals` must contain an entry with `question == q.id`); entity-name consistency lexicon (same person spelled identically across all bundles).

### 3.5 (d) Prerequisite edges — chronology ≠ prerequisite

The DAG must encode *"you can't master B without A"*, not *"A happened before B"*. What's real in this book:

**Geography — a genuine skill/concept ladder (strong edges):**
- Map-location skills (T1 U1 L1) → relief (L2) → climate & vegetation (L3): each layer literally reads the previous layer's maps, and climate regions are explained by latitude+relief.
- Climate/vegetation → population distribution (T1 U2 L2): the book explains distribution *by* climate.
- Relief + climate + population → economic activities (T2 U1: crop maps are climate-zone maps re-read).
- Everything above → country case studies (T2 U2): each country lesson is the full template (location/climate/resources/population/economy) applied — the clearest "capstone depends on all prior" node cluster in the book.
- Skill LOs (map reading, chart reading) → every map/chart-anchored LO. This is the "map skills before locating climate zones" edge, and it is the highest-fan-out prerequisite in the subject.

**History — causal-explanatory dependence (moderate edges, drawn LO-to-LO, not blanket chains):**
- The test: does a later LO's بم تفسر/استنتج question presuppose an earlier era's facts? Where yes, the edge is real *because the explanation requires it*, not because of date order. Examples the book itself asserts: Ottoman decay conditions → explaining why the French campaign found Egypt weak; French campaign results (growth of national consciousness) → explaining Muhammad Ali's popular accession («نمو الروح القومية التي أدت إلى اعتلاء محمد علي», stated in the intro, PDF 4); Muhammad Ali's state-building → successors' debt & foreign influence → Urabi → occupation → 1919 → 1936 treaty → 1952; 1952 → Palestine/1973 → peace process → 2011/2013 (T2 U3 L3 opens: «عرفت في الدرس السابق أن حرب ١٩٤٨… وعدوان ١٩٦٧ تمثل حلقات من الصراع» — the book declares the dependency).
- **Do not** edge mere adjacency (e.g. السلالات البشرية → توزيع السكان has no dependence; a 1923-constitution LO does not require the Milner-commission LO unless a comparison question spans both).

**Cross-subject edges (few but valuable):** map skills → history campaign-route LOs (خط سير الحملة maps are map-reading tasks); world political map (T1 U1 L1) → T2 U4 L2 organizations' member-state geography. Cross-course edges to math exist in principle (chart reading ↔ statistics) but skip for PoC.

Expected shape: two loosely coupled subgraphs (geo ladder, history causal chain) joined by the skills spine — a disconnected-ish DAG is fine, the validator only forbids cycles. **Estimate ~110–150 prerequisite edges** at math's observed density (112 edges / 90 LOs), skewed toward geography.

### 3.6 (e) Entities: people / places / events / organizations

The book is full of named entities (محمد علي، سعد زغلول، قناة السويس، معاهدة ١٩٣٦، جامعة الدول العربية…). Options considered:

1. **New `entity` node kind** — thesis-pure (and an `event` kind with date attributes would enable real timeline queries), but it's new schema surface, new loader logic, new UI, and nothing in the MVP tutor loop consumes entity-level retrieval. Over-engineering at pilot scale (Ch. 19.6 discipline).
2. **Model major recurring entities as `topic` nodes** with `about` edges — cheap, uses existing kinds, gives the Evidence Walk a handful of cross-lesson anchors.
3. **Keep entities inside LO descriptions and question text** — zero cost.

**Recommendation: (3) as the default, plus (2) for only the ~10–15 entities that genuinely recur across units** (محمد علي، قناة السويس، السودان، الوفد/سعد زغلول، جامعة الدول العربية، الأمم المتحدة…). Record the deferred `event`-node idea (with valid-time attributes, thesis Ch. 16) as a v2 note in the ADR — it becomes worth it the day the tutor offers "timeline of X" interactions, which the curriculum's own «خريطة زمنية» objectives suggest will come.

### 3.7 (f) Arabic content in the schema

- **Schema-ready:** `SourceDocument.language="ar"` exists; `label`/`stem`/`answer`/`solution`/`caption` are plain strings — Postgres UTF-8 handles Arabic natively. **Ids stay Latin** (`lo:ss-t1u3-2-1`, `q:ss-t1u3-2:007`, `module:ss-t2-u3`) — no change to conventions.
- **Numerals policy (decide before extraction):** the book prints Eastern Arabic digits (١٩١٩، ٧٠٫٧٪). Recommend **storing Western digits** in all data fields (searchable, sortable, comparable in code) and letting the renderer localize; Egyptian students read both fluently. Dates like «١٧٩٨–١٨٠١م» become "1798–1801م" — mixed-direction runs need bidi isolates (`<bdi>`/FSI-PDI) in the app, same class of problem the math RTL work already solved for inline LTR equations; flag for frontend regression tests on date ranges and «٢٨ فبراير ١٩٢٢م»-style strings.
- **MCQ choice keys:** keep Latin `key` values ("a"–"d") per the existing validator; display as أ/ب/ج/د in the UI (`Choice.text` is Arabic; the key→glyph mapping is a frontend concern — flag it).
- **Diacritics:** book text is largely undiacritized; store verbatim. No normalization beyond trimming tatweel; **proper-noun spelling consistency** is the real risk (see §4) — maintain a shared entity lexicon file for the extraction wave.
- **Rendering/storage implications beyond that: none.** The tutor prompts, TTS (Arabic voice pending per PROJECT_STATE), and report cards all consume these strings; Arabic-first content is the PRD's home turf — this book is actually the *first* content matching the product's target language.

### 3.8 New visual kinds needed (VIZ_SPEC v2 — the one real schema delta)

The 9 existing kinds are math-shaped. Reusable as-is: **`stat_chart`** (T2 U1's real-data bar charts drop straight in; sector charts for production shares). Everything else needs two additions:

1. **`timeline`** — the history workhorse; the book *assigns* timeline-drawing as an objective but never prints one, so this primitive adds value beyond the page. Sketch: `{range:[startYear,endYear], events:[{year, label, detail?, step:int}], animate:"sweep"}`. Covers era ordering, 1919-revolution sequence, 1936-treaty context, 1952→2013 arc.
2. **`map_scene`** — labeled schematic map: region highlight, route, point markers (continent extents, campaign routes, crop belts). Sketch: `{base:"world"|"egypt"|"africa"|"mediterranean"|…, elements:[{type:"region"|"marker"|"route"|"label", …, step:int}], animate:"sequence"}`. This is the largest build item: unlike the other primitives it needs base-map geometry in the renderer. MVP cut: ship 3–4 hand-made simplified SVG base maps (world, Egypt+Levant, Africa, Nile valley) rather than any geo library.
3. *(Optional)* `causal_chain` for أسباب→أحداث→نتائج boxes — or reuse `arrow_map` (columns = causes/results) as a stopgap; semantic mismatch is tolerable for PoC, so keep it optional.

**Dependency:** VIZ_SPEC v2 + `VIZ_KINDS` update + two renderers in `app/src/components/viz/` must land **before** visual-producing extraction waves (frontend-engineer coordination; the extraction agents produce specs against the contract, per current pipeline discipline). Question-only waves are not blocked.

### 3.9 What does NOT map cleanly — honest list

1. **No in-book questions** → no "curated seed" tier at all; the whole bank is authored. The PRD's seed-vs-variant distinction collapses for this subject; review burden is higher per question and **exam-model acquisition becomes the calibration path** (past prep-3 دراسات papers are widely available; needs a sourcing task and a rights check).
2. **Verification is agreement, not proof** (§3.4) — a genuine step down from math's re-solve guarantee; the human gate carries more weight here.
3. **Exam-policy tension**: the book disavows figures/dates as exam targets while history exam tradition still leans on them — needs an explicit authoring rule from Samuel/content reviewer before wave 1.
4. **Map-dependent knowledge**: a large share of geography facts are "read off خريطة N". Self-contained text questions can cover most, but map-reading LOs *cannot be honestly assessed without the map* → blocked on `map_scene`. Interim: tag those questions `advanced`/held-back rather than faking them as text.
5. **Politically sensitive material** (Arab–Israeli lessons, 2011/2013 revolutions, the السلالات البشرية race-classification lesson — dated framing by modern standards, but it *is* the syllabus): the tutor must stay strictly book-grounded; flag these lessons for priority human review and a note to ai-engineer that explanation prompts must not editorialize beyond the canonical text.
6. **Affective objectives** (values/قيم) exist in the official objective lists but are not assessable — they silently drop out of the LO graph; acceptable, but it means our LO count ≠ ministry objective count, worth stating in the review UI.
7. **`numeric` question type and 7 of 9 viz kinds go unused** — harmless, but dashboards that assume math's type mix will look odd; per-subject metrics should key on course id.
