Why this variant exists

A more playful variant

This variant covers ages 10–16, where feedback needs to be immediate and physical, and where a companion that visibly reacts is the difference between a session finished and a session abandoned.

Where it deliberately departs from the master brand

Stated openly, because a variant that pretends to obey everything ends up obeying nothing.

WHAT DOES NOT BEND

Playful in the details, never in the substance. No red for wrong. No leaderboards. No comparison to other students. Help is never withheld to drive engagement.

Louder does not mean manipulative. Every reward here marks something the student actually learned — never time spent, never a login.

Shape & depth

Everything is a sticker

One structural idea replaces the master system's hairline borders and soft shadows: a 3px ink outline plus a hard offset shadow, no blur. It makes every element feel like a physical object you could peel off the screen — and it gives buttons somewhere to travel when pressed.

THE PRESS
Rest
Pressed

On press the element moves +4px, +4px and its shadow collapses to zero, in 90ms. That single interaction does more for perceived responsiveness than any loading state.

RADII & STROKE

14 chips and inputs · 20 buttons and small cards · 28 big cards and sheets · pill for tags and the energy bar. Stroke drops to 2.5px on anything under 40px tall so it doesn't swallow the shape.

SPACING

Same 8px rhythm as the master system — but tap targets go up to 52px minimum, because ten-year-olds tap fast and imprecisely, often on a shared or cracked screen.

Motion

Reactive, not restless

The master system allows one spring. This variant has a vocabulary of seven — but every one of them is a response to something the student did. Nothing loops forever except the companion's breathing. Ambient animation on a study screen is noise, and it is the fastest way to make a page feel cheap.

Character

Nour has four moods, and no face

The friend mark becomes a present companion. Crucially the artwork never changes — no swapped expressions, no drawn eyebrows. Mood is carried entirely by motion, tilt, and the ring behind it, which means one asset covers every state and the character can never look like it's laughing at you.

Components

The kit

Every control is identical on web, iPad and phone — only the layout around it changes. Minimum tap target 52px everywhere, including desktop with a mouse. Every interactive element has the press state from §Shape: if it doesn't move, it isn't a control.

Buttons

Primary is always amber and always alone on the screen. Disabled loses its shadow and its outline goes dashed — it reads as "not ready yet" rather than "broken".

Screens & breakpoints

Built for the big screen first

This variant is primarily a web and iPad product. A ten‑to‑sixteen‑year‑old studies on a shared laptop at the kitchen table or an iPad on the sofa — the phone is where they check something quickly, not where a session happens. So the two‑ and three‑pane layouts below are canonical, and the phone in §Phone is the collapse of them.

The sticker kit has one property that makes this easy and one trap. Easy: outlined objects hold their identity at any size, so nothing needs re‑drawing. The trap: the sticker must not scale with the viewport. A 3px stroke and a 4px shadow are absolute values — grow them on a 27″ monitor and the interface stops looking like paper cut‑outs and starts looking like a cartoon.

DON'T · {{ r.dont }}

Web · three pane at 1360

English LTR. The extra width buys a persistent companion — Nour never has to be summoned — and a visible quest map, so the student always knows how much is left. It does not buy bigger buttons.

iPad · two pane at 1024, landscape

Arabic RTL — the rail moves to the right, the companion panel to the left, and the chat notches mirror with them. At this width the quest map is dropped rather than squeezed: the header progress bar carries that information instead.

THE ORDER OF WORK

Design the 1360 web layout first, mirror it to iPad by changing only pane widths, then collapse to phone last. Never the reverse.

Phone‑first here produces a companion that hides in a tab and a quest map that never existed — two things this variant depends on. Going the other direction, the collapse is mechanical and nothing is lost.

nour-play-rules.md
You are generating UI for Nour PLAY — the 10-16 variant of Nour, an AI
learning companion for students in Egypt and the Middle East.

THE ONE STRUCTURAL IDEA
- Everything is a sticker: 3px solid #241F3D outline + hard offset shadow
  (4px 4px 0 #241F3D, no blur). 2.5px stroke under 40px tall.
- On press: translate(4px,4px) and shadow to 0, in 90ms. If it doesn't
  move, it isn't a control.
- Radius 14 chips/inputs · 20 buttons/small cards · 28 big cards · 999 pills.

COLOR
- Ground #FFF6E6 (Cream). Surface #FFFFFF. Warm bands #FFE9BD (Honey).
- Ink #241F3D — body text AND every outline. Soft ink #6B6489.
- Action amber #F0A22F, text on it #241F3D. Mastery teal #2F9E8F.
- Celebrate violet #7B4FC9. Playmates sky #7FD1F0, leaf #B6E88F,
  berry #FFA8C5 — for SUBJECT/QUEST coding only, never decoration.
- Correct = leaf green. Wrong = grey + nudge. NEVER red, ever.

TYPE
- Baloo 2 / Baloo Bhaijaan 2 runs the WHOLE UI at 700-800: headlines,
  buttons, labels, numbers. This is the deliberate break from master.
- Cairo 400 only for passages over three lines.
- Arabic and English at equal size and weight. Arabic line-height 1.85+.

MOTION — reactive, never ambient
- press 90ms · pop 380ms spring (correct answer, reward)
- nudge 420ms (wrong answer) · wiggle (Nour thinking) · bob 3.2s loop
  (Nour idle — the ONLY infinite loop) · fill 1.4s (progress) · ring+squash
  (celebration only)
- ONE animation at a time. Nothing over 600ms. Honour prefers-reduced-motion.

THE CHARACTER
- The friend mark is a present companion in a circular outlined badge.
- FOUR MOODS carried by motion only — idle/bob, thinking/wiggle,
  encouraging/tilt, celebrating/squash+ring. NEVER redraw the artwork,
  never add facial expressions. It must never look like it's laughing at you.
- 28px inline · 44px header · 96px celebration. Under 28px, mark only.

TARGETS & LAYOUT
- Minimum tap target 52px (not 44) — fast, imprecise taps on shared phones,
  and KEEP 52px on desktop with a mouse. Density is never bought from targets.
- 8px spacing rhythm. One idea per screen. ONE amber primary per screen.

DEVICES — web and iPad are canonical, phone is the collapse
- Web 1280pt+: THREE PANE — 88px icon rail / workspace / 340px Nour panel.
  The companion panel never collapses above 1280.
- iPad landscape 1024-1194pt: TWO PANE — 80px rail / workspace / 300px Nour.
  Same component sizes as web; only pane widths change.
- iPad portrait 744-1023pt: rail + workspace, Nour docks to a bottom sheet.
- Phone / Split View under 700pt: single column, bottom nav, inline avatar.
  No intermediate layout between iPad portrait and phone.
- Design 1360 FIRST, mirror to iPad, collapse to phone LAST.

SCALING THE STICKER — the one thing that breaks on big screens
- Stroke, shadow offset and radius are ABSOLUTE. 3px stroke and 4px shadow
  at every viewport. Never scale them with the screen.
- Type does not grow either: workspace body stays 1rem, options stay 1.15rem.
  Extra width buys MORE PANES and MORE EMPTY CREAM, never bigger controls.
- Cap the workspace at 820px and text at 64ch. Cap card grids at 2 columns.
- Answer options go 1 column on phone, 2 on iPad and web. Never 3+.
- Hover styles only under @media (hover: hover) and (pointer: fine).
- RTL mirrors the rail, the panel side, and the chat bubble notches.

PROGRESS = QUESTS, NOT STREAKS
- A quest is a few questions on ONE skill, finishable in one sitting,
  labelled with its length in minutes.
- Reward only real learning: a skill reaching mastered. Never logins,
  never time spent, never the same skill twice.
- A missed day costs nothing and progress is never taken away.

FORBIDDEN
- Red for wrong answers. Leaderboards, class ranking, peer comparison.
- Streaks with loss, countdown pressure, "you'll lose your progress".
- Ambient looping animation on a study screen. Two animations at once.
- Facial expressions on the mark. Sparkle/star AI iconography.
- Withholding an explanation to drive engagement.
