Ordered pairs

QUESTION 2

If (x − 4, 7) = (9, y + 2), find the value of x × y.

Two ordered pairs are equal when their first components are equal and their second components are equal. That gives you two little equations instead of one big one, and each of them is easy on its own.

(a, b) = (c, d) <=> a = c and b = d

You got y = 5 from the second components. That bit is right.

I got x = 13 so x × y = 65?
