Point P(-5, 0) and point Q(x, 3) lie 5 units apart. Find all possible values of x.

Answer: x = -9 or x = -1

Worked solution — what the tutor teaches from
  1. Write down the distance formula and substitute the known values: dPQ=√((x-(-5))2+(3-(0))2), so 5=√((x-(-5))2+(3-(0))2).
  2. Square both sides: 25=(x-(-5))2+(3-(0))2, and (3-(0))2=9, so 25=(x-(-5))2+9.
  3. Isolate the square: (x-(-5))2=25-9=16.
  4. Take the square root of both sides: x-(-5)=±√16=±4.
  5. So x=-5±4, giving x=-9 or x=-1.
